ai-hackaton-1 Design Directions

Review-centric control surface variations built from the agreed visual and UX foundation.

D1 Tri-Pane D2 Artifact First D3 Queue + Review D4 Timeline Lens D5 Compare Desk D6 Command Hybrid

How To Evaluate

  • Does the main artifact stay visually primary?
  • Can a reviewer find blockers, open questions, and next action without hunting?
  • Does the design feel governed and calm rather than dashboard-heavy?
  • Can PM, developer, and workflow owner all use the same surface with different emphasis?
  • Does state look explainable and attributable, not merely decorative?
